## Context in one paragraph

0.7 answered "is there anything tradeable in the captured odds?" with **no** —
EPL/UCL MATCH_ODDS is an efficient, tight-spread market and neither odds-only
strategy (green-out SP-370, drift SP-371) survives costs. The lesson that drives
everything below: **to find an edge, go where the money and the bots aren't** — a
market with exploitable structure, or one less liquid than top-flight football
1X2. Full detail in `docs/SP-372_0.7_synthesis.md`. 0.8 itself is deliberately a
*foundation + investigation* release (fix capture, seed goals capture, spike
historic data); the real analysis is 0.9+, gated on data being available — which
is what makes the strategy backlog below forward-looking rather than current.

## Strategy ideas to test (once data exists) — ranked

1. **In-play Over/Under goals time-decay trading** — the standout. The fair price
   of "Over 2.5" decays predictably with time if no goal is scored and jumps
   discretely when one is. That time-decay is a genuine modelable signal, not a
   hope — unlike in-play match-odds, which 0.7 found to be event noise. Depends on
   goals capture (SP-400) accumulating, or on the historic-data path (SP-401/402).
2. **Drift-as-winner-signal, held to settlement** (drift *sense 2*) — the one
   0.7 couldn't test; needs a **results feed** (the deferred results-sourcing
   spike). SP-369 showed the pre-match drift is real; the open question is whether
   it *predicts winners* when held through the match rather than closed out before
   kick-off.
3. **Cross-market consistency** — once both match-odds and goals markets are
   captured, check whether related markets are mutually consistent (a Correct
   Score book implies a match-odds and an Over/Under book). Inconsistencies
   between *related* markets are a more realistic arb than within one efficient
   market — a smarter version of the green-out idea.
4. **Lower-liquidity competitions' match-odds** — the "same strategy, dumber
   market" play: wider spreads might let the drift edge survive costs. Cheap to
   test (re-run SP-371's sim on a lower league).

## Honest overall read

Betfair top-flight match-odds is efficient — proven. The two best bets for a
*real* edge are **(a) in-play goals markets (modelable time-decay)** and
**(b) less-liquid markets (wider spreads)**. Lead with goals markets: it's
structurally sound and the time-decay is the most genuinely predictable signal
seen so far. The capture/analysis accumulation constraint is why 0.8's smart move
is to **start goals capture + spike the historic data now** and do the real
analysis next release — so these ideas become actionable in 0.9+ once there's
data (self-captured or historic) to run them on.
